EnCryNote privacy policy

Applies to version 1.0.0-dev.3. Effective 23 October 2026.

EnCryNote is an offline notes app developed by arcahyadi. This page describes what the app does with your data, and it matches the policy shown inside the app under Settings, then Privacy policy.

No collection, no network

EnCryNote works fully offline. There is no account, no cloud sync, no telemetry, no analytics and no advertising. The app requests no network permission and contains no network code, so your notes cannot be sent anywhere.

Where your notes are stored

Notes are stored encrypted at rest in the app's private storage, using Argon2id to derive the key and AES-256-GCM to encrypt the vault. The app password and the three recovery questions and answers are chosen by you and are never transmitted.

Recovery questions are readable

Recovery questions are stored as readable header text so they can be shown while the vault is locked. Anyone with access to the device can read them, so do not put secrets in the recovery questions.

Import and export

  • Importing a .md file stores a copy encrypted in the vault. The original .md file outside the app stays plaintext and is not modified or deleted.
  • Exporting notes creates an .encrynote file protected only by the export password you choose. Recovery questions cannot open it, and a forgotten export password cannot be recovered.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app's data or uninstalling the app removes the local vault from the device. Files you exported or imported outside the app are not affected.

Third-party licences

Third-party licences are bundled with the app and can be read under Settings, then Licences.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change when the app changes. The version above is the one it describes.
